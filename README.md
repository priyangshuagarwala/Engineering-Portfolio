## Reading the portfolio

Start with a project's overview and status, then review its implementation, available evidence and limitations. This portfolio does not claim independently reproduced results unless a reproduction is explicitly documented.

## Research confidentiality

The NSFET entry includes selected terminal-characteristic plots only. Confidential research details, dimensions, geometry, structure and model files remain excluded.

## Skills demonstrated

- **Digital design:** reading and documenting Verilog dataflow, multiply–accumulate arrays and simulation/synthesis workflows.
- **Embedded systems:** register-level firmware, sensor communication, analog acquisition and bench debugging.
- **Process modeling:** layout preparation, process-sequence exploration, parameter studies and interpretation of model checks.
- **Device simulation:** organizing transfer/output characteristics, derivative plots and capacitance curves while separating exploratory output from validated conclusions.

## Evidence and limitations

Project pages explain what each artifact shows and what it does not establish. Simulation images are not fabricated-device measurements, submitted layout files are not proof of a manufactured circuit, and a hardware prototype is not a certified safety product. Unverified metrics and unfinished work are identified explicitly.
